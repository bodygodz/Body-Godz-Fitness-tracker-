# Trainer checklist sources

Items in the trainer Checklist are original short prompts. They are not quotes. These pages shaped the topics: health screening before the first workout, goals and baselines, weekly check-ins, progression, staying inside nutrition scope, CPR/AED and insurance, referrals, and keeping a certification current.

- NASM, PAR-Q overview: https://blog.nasm.org/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-par-q
- NASM, first phase with a new client: https://blog.nasm.org/off-to-a-great-start-phase-1-and-the-new-novice-client
- NASM CPT candidate handbook (scope of practice and recertification): https://support.nasm.org/hubfs/NASM%20Website/Web%20Resources/NASM%20Candidate%20Handbook%20.pdf
- ACE, simplifying assessments for new clients: https://www.acefitness.org/resources/pros/expert-articles/7436/how-to-simplify-assessments-for-new-personal-training-clients/

- ACSM Certified Personal Trainer exam content outline: https://acsm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/ACSM-Certified-Personal-Trainer-Exam-Content-Outline.pdf
- ACSM candidate handbook: https://acsm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ACSM-Candidate-Handbook-July-2025.pdf
