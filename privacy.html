Privacy

Curly Gurl Fitness stores the meals, workouts, weight, and notes you save so you and your trainer can see them.

Health data

The phone app can read steps, workouts, active calories, heart rate, sleep, and weight from Apple Health on iPhone and from Health Connect on Android. Watches such as Apple Watch, Wear OS, and Samsung Galaxy Watch put those readings in that health store. The app copies the numbers you allow into your diary.

The app does not sell health data. It does not write new readings back to Apple Health or Health Connect. The website in a browser cannot read Apple Health or Health Connect. On the website you can type those numbers yourself.

Account

When you are signed in, your diary can sync to the Curly Gurl Fitness project in Supabase for your account. Connection status for a watch is saved on your profile. Access tokens for Strava, Garmin, and Fitbit, when those connections are turned on, stay on the server and are not placed in the app page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Help row in the app.
