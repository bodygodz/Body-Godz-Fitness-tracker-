Train here, and come home to it.

Curly Gurl Fitness isn't just a brand, it's a family. Trainers, clients, and anyone walking in for the first time, you have a place here. Welcome home. That is Curly Gurl Fitness.

For the people who train here.

Curly Gurl Fitness is this family's app. This page does not borrow another gym's name, and it does not invent a headcount.

The session goes on their phone.

A trainer writes the workout, sends a video that stays with that person, and puts the next class on the calendar.

  • Workouts are built in the app and kept on the phone.
  • A video can be sent to one client.
  • Classes and bookings sit on the calendar.

A reason to open the app again.

Today holds the workout, the meals, and the water. A check-in holds photos and weight. A points feed and a comment thread are not open.

  • Today shows calories, the workout, and water.
  • Check-in stores photos and weight for that person.
  • Badges stay empty. There is no community feed.

The trainer's day, in one app.

Add a client, copy the invite code, and follow that person's weigh-ins and photos from the trainer overview. Selling products is not open. Payments are not open.

CGFitness, on the phone they already open.

The wordmark at the top is the one already in the app. The drawing is a kettlebell and a phone for this page.

Meals and macros.

Log breakfast through snacks. The day shows calories, carbs, fat, and protein. Search a food, scan a barcode, or open the cookbook. That is the meal planner.

Payments are not open. This page has nothing for sale and no price list.

Independent trainers

For a coach training on their own. Sign up as a trainer, add a client, and copy the invite code. They sign in on their own phone and join with that code.

Studios

For a room that runs classes. A group session, a training session, and the calendar are open. A separate multi-location studio tool is not.

Start with your email.

Get started opens sign-up. Trainer and client need two different emails. This is not a free trial, and there is no price list to download.
